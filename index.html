medical_services Clinical Foundations & Research Vision

I am an aspiring physician-scientist and medical scholar at Shahed University of Medical Sciences (Cohort 2028), situated at the active juncture where patient bedside realities encounter high-throughput computational intelligence. My research orientation stems from daily clinical encounters, recognizing that modern medical practitioners face unprecedented cognitive load that often distances them from empathetic patient communication.

Complementing my classical medical training, I pursued specialized executive training through the Healthcare Mini-MBA program at Tehran University of Medical Sciences (TUMS) Faculty of Pharmacy, alongside rigorous training as a Stanford University AI in Healthcare Scholar. This multi-perspective grounding enables me to approach clinical challenges not as isolated physiological queries, but as complex clinical systems requiring rigorous evidence synthesis, algorithmic transparency, and seamless bedside implementation.
