Academic Collaboration Protocols

How to propose a joint clinical AI or ML paper? expand_more

Please provide a 1-page synopsis including IRB status, retrospective/prospective dataset parameters, primary clinical endpoints, and desired co-authorship structure. We prioritize multi-center pediatric and critical care studies.

What is the dispatch process for Telegram monograph orders? expand_more

Orders submitted through the Telegram desk trigger an automated invoice and encrypted PDF download link within 15 minutes. Physical copies are dispatched via national express courier within 48 business hours with tracking.

Can I request institutional letterhead verification? expand_more

Yes. Formal verification letters for international medical fellowships, residency matching, or grant agencies can be requested directly via institutional email.
